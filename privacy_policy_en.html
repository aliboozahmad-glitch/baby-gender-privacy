Privacy Policy – Baby Gender Predict

Last updated: February 1, 2025

Welcome to Baby Gender Predict. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Sections Within the App

Our app includes the following sections:

  • Baby Gender Prediction Section
  • Genetic Diseases Section
  • Genetic Traits Section
  • Baby Face Prediction Section

2. Data We Collect

First: The Three Sections (Baby Gender - Genetic Diseases - Genetic Traits)

We do not collect any personal data in these sections. We do not store information about you or your data.

Second: Baby Face Prediction Section

When you upload photos of the mother and father, we:

  • Use the photos temporarily to analyze the face and generate features
  • Delete the photos immediately after generating the result
  • Do not keep or store the photos in any way
  • Do not use the photos for artificial intelligence model training or other purposes

3. How We Use Data

We use the photos only to show you a preview image of the child's predicted face. We delete them immediately after that.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share any data or photos with any third party.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any data, there are no security risks related to personal data storage.

6. Children's Rights

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not collect children's data.

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about the Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@babygenderpredict.com
